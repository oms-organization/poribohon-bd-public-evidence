# OMS IT ↔ BD AI — Authorization & Agreement Index

## Purpose

This index identifies the authorization, proposal, agreement, and amendment records that establish or change the OMS IT ↔ BD AI working relationship.

The index is public for continuity and provenance. It does **not** publish private agreement text, signatures, financial details, personal data, or confidential attachments unless those records are intentionally made public by the authorized parties.

## Record classes

| Record type | What it establishes | Public treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Proposed relationship, scope, services, or support structure | Record existence and high-level scope when authorized; preserve original document separately |
| Authorization | Human/organizational authority to proceed | Record authority and status without exposing private credentials or personal secrets |
| Agreement | Agreed contractual relationship and obligations | Identify parties, effective status, and public scope; authoritative terms remain in the agreement |
| Amendment | Change to an existing agreement | Record the change, effective period, and superseded provisions at a high level |
| Project authorization | Permission/scope for a specific project or workstream | Link to the project evidence where public |
| Acceptance/closure | Confirmation that a defined deliverable or stage was accepted/closed | Publish only the verifiable status and reference |

## What must be recorded before major commitments

For any future agreement involving material investment, recurring operating cost, staffing, office/infrastructure commitments, or substantial development work, the public index should be able to identify—at minimum—the existence and status of records covering:

- Legal/organizational names of the parties
- Authorized representatives and authority to sign/approve
- Effective date and term
- Scope of work and exclusions
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Engineering, operational, and support responsibilities
- Infrastructure, hosting, software, and third-party service responsibilities
- Fees, salary/staffing obligations, payment schedule, and reimbursable costs where applicable
- Intellectual-property and licensing treatment
- Confidentiality and data-handling responsibilities
- Security and access responsibilities
- Change-control procedure
- Suspension/termination provisions
- Reimbursement, liability, and outstanding-obligation treatment where applicable
- Dispute/escalation mechanism
- Amendment and renewal procedure

This checklist is a documentation-control aid, not a substitute for professional legal review.

## Evidence status vocabulary

Use explicit status labels rather than guessing:

- **PROPOSED** — proposed but not established as agreed
- **UNDER REVIEW** — being reviewed by the relevant parties
- **AGREED** — parties have recorded agreement, but execution status should be stated separately when relevant
- **EXECUTED** — authoritative agreement has been executed
- **ACTIVE** — currently in effect according to the authoritative record
- **AMENDED** — changed by a later authoritative record
- **SUPERSEDED** — replaced by a later record
- **CLOSED** — relationship/work stage formally closed
- **SOURCE-WAITING** — relationship is referenced publicly, but the underlying authoritative record has not been published

## Publication rule

Do not convert a proposal into an agreement, an agreement into an endorsement, a technical relationship into ownership, or technology use into a vendor relationship without evidence.

At the same time, do not erase a real working relationship merely because the underlying contract is private. Publish the relationship category and evidence direction at the level that can be verified.

## Source boundary

Private agreements, signatures, internal approvals, confidential commercial terms, private correspondence, credentials, and other sensitive payloads remain outside this public repository unless intentionally published by the authorized parties.
