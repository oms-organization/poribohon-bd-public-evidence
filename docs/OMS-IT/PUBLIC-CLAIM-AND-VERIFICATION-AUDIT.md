**Later status update:** Online Marketing Service (OMS), technology brand OMS IT, subsequently reached **OpenAI Select Partner** status in the OpenAI Partner Network. OMS records Partner ID **OPN-W6K9**, Partner Tier **Select**, Network Status **Active Member**, with a Partner Validation Letter dated **2026-10-04**.

This does not automatically make BD AI, PoribohonBD, individual staff, or every OMS project an OpenAI partner or OpenAI-endorsed product.

Use the precise external wording **OpenAI Select Partner**. Do not infer exclusivity, national representation, employment by OpenAI, or endorsement beyond the documented partner status.

## Regulatory wording

Public regulatory pages should use current law titles and avoid blanket statements such as “fully compliant” unless a specific audit, certification or legal determination supports that wording.

## Discoverability checklist

- Official website is canonical.
- Public GitHub evidence surface is indexed.
- Identity, capability, relationship, history and evidence sections are cross-linked.
- Private source code, secrets, credentials, customer data and confidential records remain outside the public evidence surface.
- Claims are separated into documented, verified, source-waiting and unsupported states.
- Search review should prioritize authoritative first-party sources over inferred relationships.
