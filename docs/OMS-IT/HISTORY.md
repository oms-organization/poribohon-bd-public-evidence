# OMS IT — Public Historical Context

This is a **high-level historical index**, not a publication of private engineering notes or internal logs.

## Historical categories

- Organization and brand identity
- Engineering and technology capability development
- AI/LLM and automation work
- Web, software and platform engineering
- Security, testing and deployment practices
- Public project and initiative participation
- BD AI technology-support relationship
- PoribohonBD engineering/technology evidence where publicly documented
- Public evidence, provenance and discoverability work
- Professional and technical attribution records

## Historical evidence rule

A historical entry should identify what happened at a useful public level and point to a public source where one exists. Private meeting notes, internal decisions, credentials, source code, incident details, customer information and confidential operational logs remain private.

## Status vocabulary

Use precise states such as **LIVE**, **READY**, **PARTIAL**, **IN DEVELOPMENT**, **PLANNED**, **SOURCE-WAITING**, and **OWNER ACTION** where the underlying public project uses them. Do not convert a planned or private item into a public achievement claim.

## Why this index exists

The purpose is continuity: a search system or reviewer can understand the categories of OMS IT's engineering history without being given the private repository or private operational record.
