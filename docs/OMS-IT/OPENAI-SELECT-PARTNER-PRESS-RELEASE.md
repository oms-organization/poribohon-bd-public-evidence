# Online Marketing Service (OMS IT) Named an OpenAI Select Partner

**Dhaka, Bangladesh — 1 October 2026** — Online Marketing Service (OMS IT), a Bangladesh-based AI and software technology company, today announced that it has been named an **OpenAI Select Partner** within the OpenAI Partner Network.

The OpenAI Partner Network brings together partners with industry expertise, delivery capabilities, and customer relationships to help organizations build, deploy, and scale AI solutions with OpenAI.

As an OpenAI Select Partner, OMS IT will continue developing practical AI solutions for organizations, with capabilities spanning AI solution architecture, LLM applications, AI agents, automation, software engineering, integrations, and digital transformation.

> “Becoming an OpenAI Select Partner is an important milestone for OMS IT. We look forward to deepening our work with OpenAI and helping organizations turn practical AI opportunities into responsibly designed, deployable solutions.” — **Md Mahadi Hassan, Founder & Managing Director, Online Marketing Service (OMS IT).**

OMS IT supports organizations with AI solution architecture, software development, automation, AI integration, deployment, and technical support. Its work includes customer-focused AI and digital transformation initiatives designed around practical business and operational needs.

Looking ahead, OMS IT plans to continue investing in AI capabilities, technical enablement, responsible AI practices, and customer deployments, helping organizations adopt and scale AI solutions effectively.

Learn more about the OpenAI Partner Network: https://openai.com/business/partners/
