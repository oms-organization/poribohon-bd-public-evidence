# OMS IT ↔ BD AI — Relationship History

## Purpose

This document records the public, high-level history and interpretation of the working relationship between **OMS IT** and **BD AI**. Its purpose is continuity: relationship facts, roles, and terminology should be established at the time they become relevant so that later public surfaces do not have to reconstruct them from disconnected records.

This is a historical index, not a substitute for a signed agreement or private source documents.

## Relationship model

- **OMS IT** is the engineering/technology organization participating in the relationship through applicable engineering, technology, consulting, integration, and operational support activities.
- **BD AI** is a distinct Bangladesh-focused AI initiative/platform identity.
- The relationship between the two is a **working technical/technology-support relationship** where the applicable project or agreement establishes such support.
- The existence of this working relationship does not collapse the two entities into one identity.
- Third-party AI technology/provider relationships are documented separately from the OMS IT ↔ BD AI relationship.
- Technology use, compliance with applicable provider terms/policies, or technical education does not by itself create an organizational ownership or representation relationship.
