# OMS IT — Public Project Registry

This registry is a public, evidence-oriented index of selected technology projects associated with Online Marketing Service (OMS IT / OMS Organization).

## Publication boundary

This registry publishes project identity, purpose, public-facing capabilities, official/public URLs where available, and evidence-safe summaries. Private source code, credentials, secrets, internal implementation details, customer-confidential information, and proprietary core components remain private.

Project descriptions are based on repository documentation and/or public project surfaces. Inclusion here does not by itself mean that a project is endorsed, certified, approved, or operated by OpenAI or by any government body.

## OMS Digital Hub / OMS Nexus

**Repository evidence:** private OMS Organization project `oms-nexus-forge`.

OMS Digital Hub is documented as an enterprise digital infrastructure and technology marketplace platform under OMS Organization. Its planned service scope includes domain registration, web hosting, VPS and cloud services, business email, website development packages, software solutions, AI-powered services, digital marketing, and maintenance/support services.

The repository documentation describes a modular, scalable platform direction with customer and admin systems, billing/service management, security planning, AI integration roadmap, testing, deployment preparation, and developer handover requirements.

## CitizenBD

**Repository evidence:** private OMS Organization project `citizenbd-net`.

CitizenBD is documented as a Bangladesh citizen-focused digital service and information platform intended to help people discover, understand, and navigate important citizen/public-service processes. Documented information areas include birth registration, NID, passport, replacement/lost-document guidance, and navigation to appropriate official sources.

CitizenBD is not represented as a government authority and does not claim to issue government documents. Where an official decision or service is required, the project is designed to guide users toward the appropriate official process/source.

## NFF Bangladesh Connect / NFF Digital Forge

**Repository evidence:** private OMS Organization project `nff-digital-forge`.

The repository identifies the project as Nationalist Freelancing Forum (NFF Bangladesh), with the public domain `nffbd.org`. Its documented platform direction covers freelancing, student development, digital identity, career services, PWA delivery, member lifecycle and verification, digital ID/QR verification, notices and documents, training, jobs, certificates, administrative controls, APIs, auditability, and AI-ready assistance with human control for approval-sensitive decisions.

## Registry expansion

Additional OMS Organization projects will be added progressively after their project identity, purpose, public-safe description, and evidence boundaries are reviewed. A private repository name alone is not treated as proof of a public product claim.

## OpenAI relationship boundary

Online Marketing Service (OMS IT) has organization-level OpenAI Select Partner status, documented separately in this repository. That organization-level status must not be interpreted as OpenAI approval, certification, endorsement, sponsorship, or operation of the individual projects listed in this registry unless separate authoritative evidence explicitly establishes such a relationship.
