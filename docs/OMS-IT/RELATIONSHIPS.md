# OMS IT — Public Relationship Map

This document is a public relationship map. It records only relationships that are supported by a public source or an explicitly published role description.

**Public source:** https://bdaihelp.com/about/bd-ai

Public source: https://bdaihelp.com/about

## Relationship interpretation rule

Technical support, engineering work, consulting, integration, attribution, project participation, or use of a third-party AI technology do not by themselves establish ownership, parent/subsidiary status, governance authority, government endorsement, vendor endorsement, certification, accreditation, or legal partnership.

When a future contract or public record establishes a more specific relationship, update the relevant relationship entry with the exact evidence rather than changing the relationship by inference.
