# OMS IT — Public People & Role Boundaries

This page exists to separate **organization-level roles** from **person-level public attribution**.

## OMS IT organization role

OMS IT is presented as an engineering and technology support organization for applicable public initiatives. Its technical contribution does not automatically establish ownership, governance, legal authority, government status, or control of those initiatives.

## Md Mahadi Hassan

BD AI publicly lists **Md Mahadi Hassan** as:

> AI & Technology Engineer — AI Architecture, Systems & Digital Transformation

The BD AI page labels this as **Professional / Technical Attribution Only**.

This is a person-level technical attribution. It should not be merged with BD AI ownership, government representation, vendor affiliation, certification, or organizational control.

## Two attribution contexts

Where public records separately identify a person through OMS IT and through BD AI, the records should remain context-specific:

1. **OMS IT context:** engineering / technology team or consultant context, according to the applicable public or contractual record.
2. **BD AI context:** the publicly published technical attribution shown by BD AI.

These two contexts may refer to the same person without making OMS IT and BD AI the same organization.

## Publication rule

Only publish a person's name, title, role, credential, certificate, representative status, or affiliation when the corresponding public record can be checked. Do not publish private contact information, identity documents, credentials, internal HR records, or private personnel notes.
